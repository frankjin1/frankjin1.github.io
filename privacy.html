Hermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Hermes is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive data through Google APIs, solely to help the owner read, organize and send their own email and manage their own calendar and files.

Data use

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own use.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
